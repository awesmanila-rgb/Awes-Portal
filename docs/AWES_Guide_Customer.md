# AWES Customer Portal — User Guide

**For:** Customers / building representatives
**Time to cover in orientation:** ~20 minutes

---

## 1. Signing in

You will be given a **login email and password** by AWES.

1. Open the AWES link in your phone or computer browser.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. If you look after more than one property or account, you will see a **"Select an account to view"** screen. Tap the one you want. You can switch later from your Profile.

**On your phone, install it as an app:** open the menu in your browser and choose **"Add to Home Screen"**. It then opens like a normal app. *(iPhone users: this step is required if you want notifications.)*

---

## 2. The five tabs at the bottom

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | What's happening right now — your active service, anything needing your attention |
| **Units** | Every air-conditioning unit on record for your account |
| **History** | Past service reports and completed visits |
| **Tools** | Simple calculators (electricity cost, cooling size, savings) |
| **Profile** | Your details, notification settings, and account switching |

---

## 3. Booking a service

1. On **Home**, tap **"+ Book a service"**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Which unit** — pick from your list, or leave it general
   - **What's wrong** — describe the problem in your own words
   - **Urgency**
   - **Preferred date**
   - **Site access needs** — gate pass, ladder, work permit, or anything else our technician must arrange before arriving
   - **Contact person and number** on site
3. Tap **Submit**.

Our office is notified immediately.

---

## 4. What happens after you submit

Your request moves through these stages. **Home always shows where it is.**

1. **Submitted** — we've received it.
2. **Service fee proposed** *(if applicable)* — a card appears on your Home screen showing the amount. Open it and choose **Accept Fee** or **Decline**.
3. **Schedule proposed** — if your preferred date doesn't work, we propose another. Open it and **Confirm** if the new date suits you.
4. **Scheduled** — a technician is assigned and the date is set. Your service card shows the date and time from here until the visit.
5. **Preparing** — it's the day of your visit.
6. **En Route** — the technician has confirmed and is on the way.
7. **Work in Progress** — the technician has arrived and started work.
8. **Completed** — the work is finished and is being reviewed before sign-off.
9. **Closed** — the job is signed off and complete.

> Stages 4 to 9 all appear on the **same card** on your Home screen — it fills in as the work progresses rather than being replaced by a different card.

> **Important:** When something needs your response — a fee or a proposed date — a card appears on your **Home** screen beside the main status card. Nothing moves forward until you answer it.

---

## 5. Cancelling

**Before a technician is dispatched:** open the request, choose a reason from the dropdown (or "Other" and type your own), and tap **Cancel This Request**. It cancels straight away.

**After a technician is dispatched:** you can only *request* cancellation — tap **Request Cancellation**, choose a reason, and our office will accept or decline it. You can withdraw the request while it's still pending.

---

## 6. During and after the visit

- The **progress tracker** on your Home screen updates live as the technician confirms, travels, arrives and works — you don't need to refresh.
- Use the **Message** button on the active service card to ask the office anything about that job.
- When the work is finished, the technician will fill in the **service report** on their device and ask you to **sign on screen** to acknowledge the work.
- Completed reports appear under **History**.

---

## 7. Turning on notifications *(recommended)*

Without this you'll only see updates when the app is open.

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Under **Notifications**, tap **Turn on notifications**.
3. Tap **Allow** when your browser asks.

You'll then get alerts — with sound, on your lock screen — when a fee or date needs your response, when a technician is on the way, and when your service is complete.

> **iPhone:** you must first add the app to your Home Screen (Section 1). Notifications do not work in a normal Safari tab. Requires iOS 16.4 or newer.

---

## 8. Common questions

**My details are wrong.** The portal is read-only for account details — contact AWES and we'll correct them.

**I don't see a unit that we own.** Units are added by AWES. Let us know and we'll add it.

**Can more than one person use our login?** Yes, but everyone shares the same login. Ask AWES if you need separate access.

**Nothing has happened to my request.** Check Home — if a card is waiting for your response (fee or date), the job is paused until you answer.
