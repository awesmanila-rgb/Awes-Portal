# AWES Field App — Technician Guide

**For:** Field technicians
**Time to cover in orientation:** ~45 minutes

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the AWES link on your phone.
2. **Add it to your Home Screen** (browser menu → "Add to Home Screen"). Do this once — it then opens like a normal app and works better offline.
3. Sign in with your **username and password** from the office.
4. If prompted to change your password on first login, do so.

---

## 2. The five buttons at the bottom

| Button | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your day at a glance — attendance, overview, quick actions |
| **Job Orders** | Every job assigned to you |
| **Report** | Create and view service reports |
| **Finance** | DTR, cash advance, leave, liquidation, reimbursement |
| **More** | Messages, documents, your profile, logout |

---

## 3. Start of day

1. Open **Home**. The **Welcome Back** card shows your Time In and Time Out for today.
2. Tap that attendance strip to open **DTR** and record your **Time In**.

> Time In and Time Out are only recorded on the DTR screen. The Home card shows them but you cannot tap the values themselves.

**Location sharing** turns on automatically while you are timed in, so the office can see where the team is. It stops when you time out.

---

## 4. Job orders — the five stages

Open **Job Orders** and tap any card to expand it. The step tracker inside shows exactly where it stands.

| Stage | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Open** | Assigned to you, not yet responded to | Tap **Acknowledge** when you arrive on site |
| **2. En Route** | *(optional)* You're travelling | Tap **On My Way** when you set off — the customer sees it |
| **3. Acknowledged** | You're on site, work has started | This also **unlocks the Service Report** |
| **4. Completed** | Your fieldwork is done | Tap **Mark Completed** |
| **5. Closed** | Fully finalised | Done through **Close Job Order** |

### Rules that matter

- **Acknowledge unlocks the report.** You cannot file a service report for a job order you haven't acknowledged.
- **More than one technician?** Everyone assigned must Acknowledge before the job moves on, and everyone must Mark Completed before it can be closed. If you've done your part you'll see *"Waiting for the other assigned technician(s)"*.
- **Expired job orders are closed automatically.** If the scheduled date passes and nobody acknowledged it, it closes itself. You **cannot** acknowledge it or file a report against it afterwards — there is no attendance record for that day to support a visit. Ask the office to issue a new job order.

---

## 5. Filing a service report

**Report** → pick the job order → pick the equipment unit you serviced.

The form shows **one section at a time**. Fill it in, tap **Continue** at the bottom, and the next section opens. The numbered chips at the top let you jump back to any section you've already reached.

**Sections:**

1. Customer's Information — *usually filled in for you from the job order*
2. Equipment Description — *usually filled in for you*
3. **Report Summary** — trouble call, **findings**, **recommendations** ← *you normally start here*
4. Components / Parts Needed to Replace
5. Services Done
6. Operating Data
7. Installation Data
8. **Acknowledgment** — signatures

The **Save Draft / Generate & Share Report** buttons only appear when you reach Section 8. That's intentional — the report isn't ready before then.

### Signing

In Section 8, **both** signatures are captured on your device at the site:
- The **customer** signs to acknowledge the work
- **You** sign as the technician

Then tap **Generate & Share Report**.

### Several units in one visit

Open the job order and tap **"Select multiple to batch sign"** instead of one unit. Tick every unit you serviced, tap **Continue with Selected**, fill the shared details once, and sign once. The app creates a properly-numbered report for each unit automatically.

---

## 6. Closing the job order

After the report is filed, open the job order and tap **Close Job Order**. You'll see a checklist of every equipment unit:

- **All units done** → the job closes and the customer's service is marked complete.
- **Something not finished** → tick *"scope not completed"* on that unit and give a reason. The job order still closes for today, but the customer's service stays open and the office will raise a **new job order** for the remaining work.

> This is how multi-day jobs work. Each day is its own complete job order with its own signed report. You never leave a job order open overnight.

---

## 7. Finance & HR

Tap **Finance** for:

- **Attendance (DTR)** — Time In / Time Out
- **Cash Advance** — request money for a job
- **Leave** — file a leave request
- **Liquidation** — account for a cash advance you received
- **Reimbursement** — claim money you spent yourself

**Receipts:** take a **photo** of the receipt. The app shrinks and uploads it automatically. Photos only — PDFs and other file types aren't accepted.

---

## 8. Working without signal

The app keeps working offline. Anything you save is stored on your phone and uploads by itself when signal returns.

- A **yellow bar** means work is waiting for a connection. This is normal in the field.
- A **red bar** means something was **rejected by the server** and will not upload on its own. Tap **View** to see the reason.

> **Never tap "Discard"** unless the office tells you to — it permanently deletes that work. If you see a red bar, screenshot the reason and report it.

---

## 9. Notifications *(recommended)*

**More** → **Profile** → **Turn on notifications** → **Allow**.

You'll then be alerted about new job orders even when the app is closed.

---

## 10. Quick checklist for every job

- [ ] Time In on DTR
- [ ] Tap **On My Way** when you set off
- [ ] Tap **Acknowledge** when you arrive
- [ ] Do the work
- [ ] File the **Service Report**; customer signs, you sign
- [ ] **Mark Completed**
- [ ] **Close Job Order** — flag anything unfinished
- [ ] Time Out on DTR
