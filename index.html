ℹ️ How this works▾
1. New Request — Fill in the amount, purpose, and dates, then Submit for Approval. Wait for your admin's decision under "My Requests".

2. Once approved and given to you — the request moves to the Liquidate tab automatically. You cannot start a new request until this one is liquidated.

3. Liquidate — Add every item you spent on with its receipt photo, then Submit Liquidation. If your admin disapproves it, fix the flagged items and resubmit.

4. Done — once your admin approves the liquidation, any remaining balance (to return or to be reimbursed) is settled and the cycle is complete.

Reimburse Expense is separate — use it only for money you already spent out of your own pocket that was never tied to a cash advance.

ℹ️ How Job Orders Work

Every job order goes through the same four stages. Tap a job order below to expand it — a step tracker shows exactly where it stands and what to do next.

1. Open
A new job order assigned to you that you haven't responded to yet. Tap Acknowledge to accept it.
2. Acknowledged
You've accepted it and should currently be working on site. Tap Mark Completed once the fieldwork is done.
3. Completed
Fieldwork is finished. File the Service Report, then open the ticket and run Close Job Order.
4. Closed
Fully finalized. No further action needed on these.
⚠ Expired
A warning, not a stage — it just means the scheduled date passed before the job was completed. You can still Acknowledge, Complete, or Close it; check with your dispatcher/admin if unsure.

💡 Tip: tap a job order's title bar to expand it and see its step tracker, or use "Expand all" to open everything at once.

ℹ️ How to file a Service Report▾
Single report (one unit) — Tap the Job Order below, then tap the one equipment item you serviced. Fill in Sections 1–7, then sign in Section 8. That's one Service Report.

Multiple reports, one signing (batch) — If you serviced several units on the same visit for the same customer, open the Job Order and tap "Select multiple to batch sign" instead of a single item. Check every unit you serviced, then tap Continue with Selected. Fill in the shared details once (findings, services done, etc.) and sign once in Section 8 — the app automatically creates one properly-numbered Service Report per unit you checked, each carrying that same signature.
🖊️ Batch Signing Mode

Equipment details below are pulled automatically per unit — Section 2 is skipped in this mode. Fill in the shared details once, then sign in Section 8 to generate every report below at once.
