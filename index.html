How this works
1. New Request — Fill in the amount, purpose, and dates, then Submit for Approval. Wait for your admin's decision under "My Requests".

2. Once approved and given to you — the request moves to the Liquidate tab automatically. You cannot start a new request until this one is liquidated.

3. Liquidate — Add every item you spent on with its receipt photo, then Submit Liquidation. If your admin disapproves it, fix the flagged items and resubmit.

4. Done — once your admin approves the liquidation, any remaining balance (to return or to be reimbursed) is settled and the cycle is complete.

Reimburse Expense is separate — use it only for money you already spent out of your own pocket that was never tied to a cash advance.

How Job Orders Work

Every job order moves through the stages below. Tap a job order to expand it — a step tracker shows exactly where it stands and what to do next.

Scheduled
Booked ahead. You can open it and review the details. Acknowledge appears 4 hours before the scheduled time — so an 11:59 PM job opens at 7:59 PM.
1. Preparing
The window is open. Tap Acknowledge to accept the job order. On a shared job order, everyone assigned has to acknowledge before the customer is told the crew is coming.
2. En Route
Everyone has acknowledged and the customer can see you're on the way. Tap Arrived at Site when you get there — one tap from any assigned technician starts the work for the whole crew.
3. Work in Progress
Your arrival time fills in Time In on the Service Report automatically. File a report for each unit on the job order. If a unit can't be serviced, flag it as Not Yet Done and give the reason.
4. Completed
Happens on its own once every unit has either a Service Report or a Not Yet Done reason — there's no button to press. Admin then reviews it.
5. Closed
Admin has reviewed the reports and closed the job order. Nothing further is needed from you. Only admin can close a job order.
Expired
Nobody acknowledged it within 8 hours of the scheduled time, so it closed automatically and no Service Report can be filed against it — there is no attendance record to support a visit. Ask your admin to issue a new job order. A job order you did acknowledge will not expire, however long the work runs.

Tip: tap a job order's title bar to expand it and see its step tracker, or use "Expand all" to open everything at once.

How to file a Service Report
1. Start — Tap Create New, then pick the Category of work: Aircon, Ventilation or General Scope. Only job orders under that category are listed.

2. Pick the Job Order — Tap the job order you're on. You must have tapped Acknowledge on it in My Job Order first, and someone must have tapped Arrived at Site.

3. Single or Multiple — Single Report: tap the one unit you serviced. Multiple Reports: check every unit that got the same works done, then tap Continue with Selected — you fill the shared details once (Equipment Details is skipped, Operation Parameters are entered per unit) and the app creates one numbered report per unit.

4. Fill each step — Tap Continue at the bottom of each step; only one step shows at a time. Use the chips at the top to go back to an earlier step.

5. Sign — You sign in the Technician Signature step, then the customer signs in Customer Acknowledgment. Tap Confirm Signature on each to lock it in.
